# 300CC V2 – Open-Source Paste Extruder (LDM)

The **300CC V2** is an open-source extruder designed by *Emmanuel Hugnot* to make paste 3D printing practical, reliable, and accessible. Built as the evolution of the original *300CC* project, it allows anyone to experiment with materials like **clay, earth, or bio-composites** without needing expensive hardware.

By focusing on a stripped-down design where *every single component serves a clear purpose*, the entire extruder can be assembled for **under €100**.

---

## Key Features

* **Budget-Friendly:** Full assembly costs *less than €100*.
* **Minimalist Design:** Optimized geometry for straightforward assembly, easy maintenance, and reliable extrusion.
* **Versatile Materials:** Ideal for *clays, ceramic bodies, bio-materials*, and other ductile/viscous pastes.
* **FDM to LDM Conversion:** Designed specifically to help users convert standard desktop FDM 3D printers into **Liquid Deposition Modeling (LDM)** machines.

---

## Project Origins & Acknowledgments

The **V2 design** was refined in *September 2026* during a collaborative workshop with **3rd-year design students at ESAD Orléans**. This hands-on collaboration helped test, iterate, and streamline the assembly process to ensure it remains user-friendly and functional.

## Getting Started

1. **Check the BOM:** Review the `Bill_of_Materials/` folder for the complete list of required hardware, fasteners, and electronics.
2. **Print the Parts:** Download the `.STL` files from `3D_Models/` and slice them using recommended settings in the documentation.
3. **Assemble & Mount:** Follow the step-by-step guide in `Documentation/Assembly_Guide.md` to adapt the 300CC V2 to your printer frame.
